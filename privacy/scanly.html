Privacy Policy — Scanly

App: Scanly — QR & Barcode Scanner (com.tvttechnology.scanly)
Last updated: June 11, 2026

TVT Technology ("we", "us") built Scanly as a free app. This page explains what information the app handles and how it is used. By using Scanly, you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

Scanly does not require an account and we do not collect, store, or sell your personal information on our own servers.

  • Camera: Scanly uses your device camera solely to scan QR codes and barcodes. Images are processed on your device in real time and are not uploaded or stored by us.
  • Scan history: Your scan results may be saved locally on your device so you can review them later. They never leave your device unless you choose to share them.

Advertising

Scanly displays ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use data such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, and approximate location to show ads (including personalized ads where permitted). You can learn more and control ad personalization here:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses data in AdMob
  • Google ad settings

Third-party services

The app may use third-party services that collect information used to identify your device for analytics, crash reporting, or advertising purposes. These services operate under their own privacy policies, including Google Play Services and Google AdMob.

Links scanned with the app

When you open a link from a scanned code, you leave the app and are subject to the destination site's own terms and privacy practices. Always check links from unknown sources before opening them.

Children's privacy

Scanly is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Since we do not store your personal data on our servers, your scan data stays on your device. We still recommend keeping your device and the app up to date.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
